RECONSTRUCTED EVIDENCE — SAVED TROUBLESHOOTING RECORD

Repeatable pattern:
1. PC/display enters sleep.
2. PC wakes / appears powered.
3. Monitor reports No DisplayPort Signal.
4. RX 580 may fail to initialize or disappear.
5. Forced shutdown / full power cycle restores it.

Workaround used:
- No PC sleep
- No monitor/display sleep
- Full shutdown instead

Supported conclusion:
Sleep/power-state transitions were a strong trigger.
